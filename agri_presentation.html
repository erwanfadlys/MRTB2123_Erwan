Strategic Value

Strategic Impact & Contribution to SDG 2

SDG 2 · Zero Hunger
RE

Resource Efficiency

The sunflower irrigation-nitrogen study found that yield and resource-use efficiency improved inside an optimal ET and nitrogen window, showing why precision systems should target thresholds instead of simply maximising inputs (Chen et al., 2024).

YF

Yield Forecasting

The yield-prediction reviews show that earlier forecasting supports better harvest planning, storage preparation, and input timing before the season closes (Shawon et al., 2025; Saha et al., 2025).

SI

Sensor-Driven Intervention

Recent sensor studies emphasise continuous monitoring of moisture, nutrient status, and crop condition so farms can intervene earlier and more precisely within the season (Mansoor et al., 2025; Eze et al., 2025).

S2

SDG 2 Pathway

Across these papers, the clearest contribution to Zero Hunger is more reliable crop output from better-timed water and nutrient decisions, not from blanket increases in fertiliser or irrigation (Saha et al., 2025; Chen et al., 2024).

Interaction Modelling: Irrigation x Nitrogen Response

Y = Crop Yield (t/ha)
ET = Seasonal ET / water input (mm)
N = Nitrogen (kg/ha)
★ Interaction Logic ET×N
The 2024 sunflower study shows that water and nitrogen should be optimised jointly, because yield gains taper off once the optimum zone is exceeded (Chen et al., 2024).

Business Analytics Lens

Business Analytics Summary: From Farm Data to Decision Value

DA

Descriptive Analytics

Sensor, weather, soil, and imagery streams create a current-state view of field conditions, input use, and crop performance for routine monitoring.

DG

Diagnostic Analytics

Interaction analysis helps explain why yields differ across plots by linking performance gaps to irrigation, nitrogen timing, and environmental variation.

PA

Predictive Analytics

Yield forecasting turns early-season signals into forward-looking estimates that support harvest planning, procurement, logistics, and revenue expectations.

PS

Prescriptive Analytics

Optimisation models convert predictions into recommended actions, helping managers allocate water and fertiliser where they deliver the strongest operational and sustainability return.

Precision Analytics:The Future of Sustainable Agriculture

The verified literature points to the same sequence: sensors improve field visibility, machine learning improves yield forecasting, and optimisation improves irrigation and nitrogen timing. Together, these mechanisms support SDG 2 through more reliable crop output and better resource efficiency (Shawon et al., 2025; Mansoor et al., 2025; Chen et al., 2024; Singha et al., 2026).
